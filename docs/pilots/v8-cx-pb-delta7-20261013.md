# V8 real-data research pilot: PB EPG–Delta7–PEN wiring

## Question

Study only *Drosophila* central-complex anatomy. In hemibrain v1.1,
does an EPG -> Delta7 -> PEN route within the protocerebral bridge (PB)
reach different numbers of PEN target neurons compared with the direct
EPG -> PEN connection? Quantify type-specific direct vs two-hop reachability.
Both positive and negative descriptive outcomes are acceptable.

Biological basis: Hulse et al. (2021), eLife 10:e66039,
https://elifesciences.org/articles/66039 (EPG/PEN loop and Figure 20,
Delta7 neurons). This narrow structural reanalysis must not be portrayed
as a new proof of an attractor, a calcium-imaging experiment, or a
physiological test.

## Estimand

For each EPG neuron e and separately each population T in {PEN_a, PEN_b}:
- DIRECT: set of target T neuron IDs p with a positive PB edge e->p.
- VIA_DELTA7: set of target T neuron IDs p with at least one real Delta7
  neuron d satisfying positive PB edges e->d AND d->p.
- Compute cardinality, overlap, fraction of all T neurons and the paired
  fractional difference (via - direct), retaining zero-coverage EPGs.
- Aggregate separately by target population; show per-neuron distributions,
  type counts, relevant type-pair edge counts and total synapse weights.
  Do not equate a two-hop path or synapse count to neural signal gain.

Do not invent PB glomerulus geometry, neuron angular position, synaptic sign
or effective function. No artificial randomized baselines, no p-values
treating neurons from one connectome as independent animal samples.
This is a narrow, descriptive connectomic analysis that follows existing
published anatomical findings.
